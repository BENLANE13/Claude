Our story הסיפור שלנו

Breakfast doesn't
have a bedtime.ארוחת בוקר בכל שעה

Tel Aviv eats breakfast like it means it: shakshuka at noon, sabich after the beach, a falafel at 3 a.m. We wanted all of that, in one hand, in one foil wrapper.

So we set up a tiny kitchen under the ficus trees of Rothschild Boulevard and started rolling. Eggs scrambled soft to order, potatoes fried until they shatter, cheese melted on the griddle, and salsas we blend fresh every morning at five.

Early-shift bakers, night-shift nurses, DJs on their way home, Bauhaus-loving tourists: if you're hungry between sunrise and 2 a.m., you're our people.
