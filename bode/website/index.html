How it works

Three taps from sun to shade.

1 · Unlock

Find a BODE on the map

Open the app, see drones parked on Roosts across campus, and tap to unlock. The nearest one launches and flies to you in under a minute.

2 · Walk

It holds the angle for you

BODE tracks you with its cameras and GPS, predicts your next step, and keeps its 1-meter canopy on the line between your head and the sun. Your route is logged as shade-minutes for your campus.

3 · Release

It flies itself home

End the ride and BODE returns to the closest Roost to charge in the sun. No parking, no clutter on the sidewalk, no scooters in the bushes.

Campus Wars

BODE Link · for partners

Plugs into the apps people already ride.

Micromobility apps like Lime run on open data standards. BODE speaks them too, so a scooter app can show BODEs on its map, book an escort for the walk after a ride, and share Campus Wars points.

Open fleet feed

BODE publishes a standard GBFS feed of every Roost and available drone, the same format scooter and bike fleets use. Any trip planner can list us.

/gbfs/gbfs.json

Escort API

A partner app requests a BODE for its rider with one call. We send live status by webhook: dispatched, arrived, shading, returned.

POST /v1/escorts

Safety

Built to fly near people.

Guarded rotors

Ducted props sit outside the canopy and never over your head. Soft foam rim on every edge.

Remote ID on every drone

Every BODE broadcasts FAA Remote ID and flies inside geofences approved by each campus.

Human oversight

Licensed Part 107 pilots monitor the fleet from our ops center and can take over any drone.

Weather aware

BODE stays on its Roost in high wind, rain, and lightning, and lands itself if conditions change mid-ride.

Privacy first

The tracking camera finds you on-device. No video leaves the drone, and you can delete your route history anytime.

Quiet hours

Campus-set no-fly times around classes, exams, and residence halls.
