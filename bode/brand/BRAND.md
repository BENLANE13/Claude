# BODE Brand

**Name:** BODE (all caps in the wordmark; "Bode" in running text is also fine)
**Tagline:** Shade that follows you.
**Product idea in one line:** The angle that shades you is the angle that charges it.

## Color
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Eclipse Violet** | `#6B3BFF` | The brand color: every drone canopy, logo, primary buttons |
| Violet (dark UI) | `#8A63FF` | Brand color on dark backgrounds |
| Halo | `#B9A3FF` | Shadow in the logo, soft accents |
| Night Ink | `#17122E` | Text, dark surfaces |
| Haze | `#F3F1FA` | Light background |
| Sun Amber | `#FFB020` | The sun only, never for UI chrome |

Why violet: Lime is green, Bird is black, Spin is orange, and Veo is teal and green. Violet is unclaimed in micromobility, reads as "cool" against hot sun, and stands out in the sky.

## Logo
- `logo-mark.svg`: app icon / avatar. The sun, a canopy tilted toward it (cutting into the sun like an eclipse), and a person in its shadow.
- `logo.svg`: mark plus wordmark.
- Keep clear space equal to the canopy's height around the mark. Don't recolor the mark except to all-white on violet or photos.

## Type
- Display: **Unbounded** (wide, engineered, aerial)
- Body: **Instrument Sans**
- Data and readouts: **JetBrains Mono**

## Voice
Plain, warm, and a little playful. Talk about walks, sun, and shade, not drones and specs. Say "unlock", "walk", "release" to riders. Save the engineering for partners and investors.
