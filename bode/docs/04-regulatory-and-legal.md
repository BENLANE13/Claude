# Regulatory and Legal

> This is a planning checklist, not legal advice. Have an aviation attorney review it before operating.

## FAA (United States)
| Requirement | What it means for BODE |
|---|---|
| **Part 107** (commercial small UAS) | BODE operates commercially, so a certificated remote pilot must oversee operations. Plan for an ops center with Part 107 pilots. |
| **Operations over people (Part 107 Subpart D)** | Category 1 requires under 0.55 lb, which BODE can't meet. Target **Category 3**: a declaration of compliance, limits on impact energy, no exposed lacerating rotors, and restrictions (e.g. closed or restricted-access sites, or people who have been notified). Campus geofences and rider consent support this. Confirm the current text in eCFR 14 CFR 107.39 and 107.100–107.165. |
| **Remote ID (Part 89)** | Required for registered drones and all Part 107 operations. Build standard Remote ID into every BODE. |
| **Registration** | Every drone registered with the FAA. |
| **Beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) and one-to-many operations** | One pilot supervising many drones needs a waiver or exemption (or a future BVLOS rule). Plan a waiver application with a safety case. Pilot phase: 1 pilot per 5 drones. |
| **Airspace** | Many campuses sit near airports (controlled airspace) and need LAANC/DroneZone authorization. Check each campus before signing. |

## Campus and local
- Written operating agreement with each university: geofences, quiet hours, Roost locations, indemnity.
- Check state and city drone ordinances (some restrict flights over parks or events).
- Stadium and event flight restrictions (TFRs on game days).

## Privacy
- Vision processing on-device only; no video stored or uploaded.
- Route history: rider-deletable, retained 90 days by default, aggregated for Campus Wars.
- Privacy policy covering location data; CCPA/state-law compliance; FERPA does not apply (data isn't an education record), but avoid sharing data with universities at the individual level.

## Company setup
- Delaware C-corp (standard for venture funding), with founder vesting (4 years, 1-year cliff) and IP assignment.
- Trademark search and filing for **BODE** (Class 12 vehicles/drones, Class 39 transport/rental services, Class 9 software). Search USPTO first; "BODE" may have existing marks.
- Provisional patent on combined shade-positioning and solar-tracking control.
- Insurance: aviation liability, product liability, general liability, cyber.
- Terms of service with rider acknowledgment of drone operations.
