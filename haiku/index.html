Our Story

Born from the Rothschild Vibes

We noticed something missing on Rothschild: a place that takes breakfast seriously. No rushed decisions. No disappointing eggs. Just massive, carefully-built burritos loaded with real ingredients, made fresh to order while you wait.

We pull inspiration from Tel Aviv's street food culture—bold flavors, fresh takes on classics, a touch of Middle Eastern soul. Whether you're stumbling in at 2 AM after a night out or grabbing breakfast before the beach, we've got the burrito that fits your morning.

Sababa (סבבה) is Hebrew for "cool" or "great"—and that's exactly what we're trying to bring to breakfast.
