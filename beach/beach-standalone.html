How the score works

Each beach gets a 0–100 score from the hourly forecast, averaged over beach hours (10am–5pm, remaining hours if it's already afternoon).

  • Air temp 20% – best 76–88°F
  • Water temp 15% – 70°F+ is comfy, below 60°F is brutal
  • Waves 15% – calm to small (≤3 ft) is best for swimming
  • Wind 15% – under 8 mph is best
  • Sky 15% – sun and low rain chance
  • UV 10% – very high UV lowers the score a bit and triggers a sunscreen warning
  • Air quality 10% – US AQI

Surf score (shown on every card): swell height 40% (4–6 ft is ideal; a rough 0.3–0.5× factor for breakwater-sheltered Long Beach and Cabrillo), swell period 25% (12s+ is best), and wind 35% (offshore or light is glassy; onshore wind hurts). Wind direction is judged against which way each beach faces. Tide isn't factored in.

Data: Open-Meteo (weather, marine model, air quality/UV) and NOAA CO-OPS (tides, LA Outer Harbor). Drive times from USC are rough estimates. Wave/water data is a model, not a lifeguard report — always check posted flags and rip-current warnings.
