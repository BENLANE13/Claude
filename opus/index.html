Our story הסיפור

A burrito for every shift of the city.

גִּלְגּוּל (gil-GOOL) means a roll — and also a rebirth. That's the whole idea: every morning breakfast gets reborn, rolled tight in a hot flour tortilla and handed through our window under the ficus trees of Rothschild.

We started making burritos for friends after night shifts in bars and hospitals, then for the sunrise runners heading to the beach. The recipe stayed simple: soft eggs, crispy potatoes, melty cheese, a salsa that bites back — and a few things only Tel Aviv would do, like amba, schug and sabich in a tortilla.

Grab one on the bench, take it to the beach, or let it come to you. Either way, it'll be the best thing you eat before noon. Or after midnight.

How to order איך מזמינים

Hungry? Three steps to foil.

Walk up to the window, order ahead for pickup, or stay in bed and let a courier do the walking.

Pick your roll

Choose a burrito, a salsa and any add-ons. Can't decide? The Rothschild never misses.

We roll it, fast

Eggs hit the griddle when you order — about 4 minutes from crack to foil, even at 3am.

Pickup or delivery

Grab it hot from our window, or get it delivered across central Tel Aviv in about 25 minutes.

Delivered via Wolt

Search "Gilgul Rothschild" in the Wolt app. Delivering daily 07:00–03:00 to Lev Ha'ir, Neve Tzedek, Florentin, the Old North & Jaffa. Burritos travel in double foil, so they arrive hot and intact.

Order ahead, skip the line

Call or WhatsApp the window and your foil-wrapped bundle will be waiting with your name on it.
